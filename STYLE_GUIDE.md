## Color Theme

### Primary Colors
- Dark Blue-Gray: `#2c3e50` - Headers, text, navigation
- Red: `#c0392b` - Buttons, active states, accents
- Light Gray: `#f8f9fa` - Page background
- Off-White: `#ecf0f1` - Card backgrounds, hover states
- White: `#ffffff` - Content areas

### Text Colors
- Primary Text: `#2c3e50`
- Secondary Text: `#7f8c8d` 

---

## Typography

- Font Family: `'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Geneva, Verdana, sans-serif`

### Font Sizes:
- Headings: `1.5rem` to `2rem`
- Body text: `1rem`
- Small text: `0.85rem` to `0.95rem`

### Font Weights:
- Light (300): Taglines
- Normal (400): Body text
- Medium (500): Navigation, labels
- Semi-Bold (600): Headings, buttons

---

## Spacing & Layout

- Container max-width: `1200px`
- Border radius: `8px` (small), `12px` (large)
- Standard spacing: `1rem` to `2rem`

---

## Component Styles

### Buttons
- Background: `#c0392b`
- Text: White
- Border radius: `8px`
- Hover: Darker red (`#a93226`)

### Forms
- Input border: `2px solid #ecf0f1`
- Input background: `#f8f9fa`
- Focus border: `#2c3e50`
- Error border: `#c0392b`

### Cards
- Background: White
- Border radius: `12px`
- Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`

---

## Responsive Design

**Mobile breakpoint**: `768px`
- Navigation becomes vertical
- Reduced padding and font sizes
